how it works

three steps. that’s it.

  1. Connect your bank. Read-only, via Plaid.

  2. See your month, company by company.

  3. Invest later. Preview only, not built yet.

what your bank sends us

  • The date, amount and merchant of each purchase.
  • Which account it came from: name and last four digits.
  • The update when a pending charge settles.

what it never asks for

  • A way to move money. There is no code that does.
  • Your banking password. You type it at your bank or Plaid.
  • To keep reading after you disconnect.

Not public yet. One email when it opens.

the fine print

Example numbers. Not real data. Every receipt, total and figure on this page comes from a made-up month.
