The same number, resorted.

The reading that unsettles and the reading that pleases are built from the same purchases.

  1. Connect banks.

    Through Plaid, which asks your bank for transactions and nothing more. Several banks and all their cards, pooled into one picture.

  2. Read the month.

    Your purchases, added up, with money you moved between your own accounts taken back out. We match the pair: same amount, within four days, out of a current account and into a card. It is our inference and it can be wrong, so every removal is listed with its reason.

  3. Then the treat, later.

    The same purchases, sorted into the listed companies behind them. Coming later. The spending half works today; the investing half is a preview on this page.
