Connect banks.

Several banks and all their cards, pooled into one picture. Trick or Treat asks Plaid for transactions only, and has no code that moves money.

Track spending.

Your own purchases added up, with the money you moved between your own accounts taken back out. If the number surprises you, that is the number doing it. It can be wrong: pending charges move, and matching your own transfers is our inference.
